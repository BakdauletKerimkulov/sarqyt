Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

Sarqyt ("we", "us", "our") operates the Sarqyt mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use the App.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, profile photo.
  • Location data: your device's GPS coordinates, used to show nearby offers. Location is only accessed with your explicit permission and is not stored on our servers.
  • Order data: order history, items purchased, pickup times, payment status.
  • Payment data: payment method details are processed by our payment provider (Kaspi Pay / Stripe). We do not store full card numbers.
  • Device data: device type, operating system, push notification token.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the App's services.
  • To process orders and payments.
  • To display nearby food offers based on your location.
  • To send push notifications about order status updates.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To prevent fraud and ensure security.

3. Data Sharing

We share your data only in the following cases:

  • With partner stores: your name and order details are shared with the store fulfilling your order.
  • With payment providers: payment data is processed by Kaspi Pay and/or Stripe under their respective privacy policies.
  • With Firebase (Google): we use Firebase for authentication, database, cloud functions, and crash reporting.
  • As required by law: we may disclose data if required by applicable legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Cloud (Firebase) infrastructure. We use industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Order history is retained for 3 years for legal and accounting purposes. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Your Rights

Under the laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan ("On Personal Data and Its Protection"), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@sarqyt.kz.

7. Location Data

The App requests access to your device's location to show nearby food offers and calculate distances. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. The App will continue to work but without location-based features.

8. Push Notifications

We send push notifications about order status changes (e.g., "Your order is ready for pickup"). You can disable notifications in your device settings or in the App's settings.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us:
